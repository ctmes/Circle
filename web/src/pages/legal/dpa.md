## 1. How this fits together

This Data Processing Addendum (**"Addendum"**) forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between <span class="todo">[Legal entity name] Pty Ltd</span> (**"Processor"**, **"we"**) and the customer (**"Controller"**, **"you"**). Where this Addendum and the Terms disagree about the processing of Personal Data, this Addendum prevails.

It applies to the extent we process Personal Data on your behalf in providing the Service. It does not apply to personal information we hold as controller in our own right — our own users' account records, our billing records, our correspondence with you — which is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

**Definitions.** *Personal Data*, *processing*, *data subject*, *controller*, *processor*, *supervisory authority* and *personal data breach* have the meanings given in the GDPR. *Personal information*, *APP entity* and *eligible data breach* have the meanings given in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth). *Data Protection Law* means the Privacy Act and the APPs, the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and any other privacy law applicable to a party's processing under this Addendum.

## 2. Roles and instructions

You are the controller and we are the processor. Where a Circle spans several organisations, each party is a controller of the Personal Data it contributes, and the convener is a controller of the Circle's own structural records. Nothing in this Addendum makes us a joint controller with you.

We will process Personal Data only:

- on your documented instructions, which are the Terms, this Addendum, the configuration you set in the Service (including party structure, access rules, agent-readable flags and agent mandates), and any further written instruction you give; and
- where a law we are subject to requires otherwise, in which case we will tell you before processing unless that law forbids it on important grounds of public interest.

**You warrant** that you have a lawful basis for the Personal Data you put into the Service, that you have given the notices your own law requires, and that your instructions do not put us in breach of Data Protection Law. If we consider an instruction infringes Data Protection Law, we will tell you promptly and may suspend that processing until it is resolved.

**We will not** use Personal Data for our own purposes; sell it; disclose it for advertising; or use it to train, fine-tune or improve any machine learning model. We impose the same restriction on every sub-processor.

## 3. Confidentiality and our people

Access to Personal Data is limited to personnel who need it to perform our obligations. Every person with such access is under a written duty of confidence that survives their engagement, receives training appropriate to their role, and has access removed promptly when it is no longer needed.

## 4. Security

We implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures described in **Annex 2**, having regard to the state of the art, the cost of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, as well as the risk to individuals.

We may change those measures over time, provided the level of protection does not materially decrease.

## 5. Sub-processors

You give general written authorisation for us to engage sub-processors. Each is engaged under a written contract imposing data protection obligations no less protective than this Addendum, and we remain fully liable to you for their performance.

The current list is published at [/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors). We will give at least **30 days' notice** of an intended addition or replacement by updating that page and emailing the address you nominate for the purpose. If you have a reasonable objection on data protection grounds, tell us within that period and we will work with you in good faith on an alternative. If we cannot find one, you may terminate the affected part of the Service without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

## 6. Assisting you

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us, we will assist you with:

- **Data subject requests.** The Service lets you access, export, correct and delete the content in a Circle directly. If a request reaches us instead, we will not respond to it substantively; we will pass it to you without undue delay and help you answer it.
- **Security, breach notification and impact assessments** — Art. 32 to 36 of the GDPR and the equivalent obligations under the Privacy Act.
- **Records.** We maintain a record of the categories of processing carried out on your behalf and will make it available on request.

Where the assistance requested goes materially beyond what the Service already provides, we may charge a reasonable fee, agreed with you first.

## 7. Personal data breaches

We will notify you **without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours**, of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Personal Data we process for you. The notice will describe, so far as we know it: the nature of the breach and the categories and approximate numbers of records and individuals involved; the likely consequences; the measures taken or proposed; and a contact point for more information. Where we cannot provide all of it at once, we will provide it in stages without further undue delay.

We will not notify a supervisory authority, a regulator or any data subject on your behalf unless you ask us to in writing or the law requires us to. You remain responsible for your own notification obligations, including under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and Art. 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

## 9. Audit

We will make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum, including <span class="todo">[our most recent third-party security report, once one exists]</span> and completed security questionnaires.

Where that is not sufficient, you may audit — yourself or through an independent auditor who is not our competitor and who signs a confidentiality undertaking — on at least **30 days' notice**, no more than once in any twelve months unless a regulator requires more or we have had a breach affecting your data. Audits happen during business hours, must not unreasonably disrupt our operations, must not involve access to other customers' data, and are at your cost.

## 10. Return and deletion

On termination, and on your written request at any time, we will delete or return Personal Data as set out in clause 14 of the Terms: an export window, deletion from production, then deletion from backups as they expire on their ordinary cycle.

We may keep Personal Data where a law requires it or where it is under legal hold, and in that case we keep it only for that purpose, protected by this Addendum for as long as we hold it, and delete it when the requirement ends.

## 11. Liability

Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations and exclusions in clause 12 of the Terms, except where Data Protection Law does not allow it to be limited.

## Annex 1 — Details of processing

**Subject matter.** Provision of the Circle platform: a bounded, time-limited workspace for cross-company collaboration on a defined outcome.

**Duration.** For the term of the Terms, plus the export and deletion windows in clause 14 of the Terms.

**Nature and purpose.** Hosting, storage, transmission, indexing and retrieval of customer content; text extraction from documents, images, audio and video; access control and audit logging; generating drafted claims, summaries and decision requests where the customer has enabled an agent; producing an export packet on closure.

**Types of Personal Data.** Determined by you. Typically: names, work email addresses, job titles, organisational affiliations, Circle roles, authentication and session records, activity and audit records — and whatever personal data appears within the content you upload, which may include names, contact details, signatures, images of identifiable people in site photography, and voices in recorded audio.

**Special categories.** Not expected, and not requested. If you intend to process special category data through the Service, tell us first so that we can agree the additional measures Art. 9 requires.

**Categories of data subjects.** Your personnel; personnel of other parties to a Circle; individuals identifiable within uploaded evidence.

**Frequency of transfer.** Continuous, for the duration of the agreement.
